Whiskey Basics: Bourbon, Rye and More

Paola Market • Paola, Kansas

Whiskey is a broad category with styles shaped by grain, place, production methods, and aging.

Bourbon

An American whiskey made from a grain mixture containing at least 51% corn and aged in new charred oak containers. Common flavor notes include caramel, vanilla, oak, and baking spice.

Rye

American rye whiskey uses at least 51% rye in the grain mixture and often tastes spicier or drier than bourbon.

Scotch and Irish whiskey

These categories follow their own regional production rules and can range from light and approachable to rich, smoky, or complex.

How to explore

Try small pours side by side, add a few drops of water if desired, and pay attention to aroma, sweetness, spice, oak, and finish.
