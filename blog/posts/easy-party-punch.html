Easy Party Punch for a Small Gathering

Paola Market • Paola, Kansas

Batch drinks can make hosting easier because you can prepare most of the work before guests arrive.

Simple citrus punch

  • 2 cups spirit of choice
  • 1 cup fresh citrus juice
  • 3/4 cup simple syrup
  • 3 cups chilled sparkling water or soda
  • Citrus slices and plenty of ice

Method

  1. Combine the spirit, citrus, and syrup in a pitcher.
  2. Chill before serving.
  3. Add ice and sparkling mixer shortly before guests arrive.
  4. Taste and dilute slightly if needed.

Serve water and nonalcoholic choices alongside the punch, and encourage guests to plan safe transportation.

21+ only. Please enjoy responsibly and never drink and drive.
