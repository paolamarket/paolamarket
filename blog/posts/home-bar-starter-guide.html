A Simple Starter Guide to Building a Home Bar

Paola Market • Paola, Kansas

You do not need dozens of bottles to make a useful home bar. Start with versatile categories and expand based on the drinks you actually enjoy.

A practical foundation

  • One whiskey
  • One vodka or gin
  • One tequila or rum
  • One orange liqueur or versatile modifier
  • Aromatic bitters
  • Fresh citrus and a simple sweetener

Add based on your favorites

If you make Margaritas often, build around tequila and citrus. If whiskey drinks are your favorite, add a second whiskey style or sweet vermouth. Your home bar should reflect what you actually drink.

21+ only. Please enjoy responsibly and never drink and drive.
