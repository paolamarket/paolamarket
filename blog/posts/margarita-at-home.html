A Fresh Margarita You Can Make at Home

Paola Market • Paola, Kansas

A fresh Margarita is bright, citrusy, and easy to remember once you know the basic ratio.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz tequila
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice
  • 3/4 oz orange liqueur
  • Optional: 1/4 oz agave syrup
  • Ice and lime wheel

Method

  1. Add all ingredients to a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake until well chilled.
  3. Strain into a glass over fresh ice, or serve up.
  4. Garnish with lime. Salt the rim if you like.

Quick adjustment

If your limes are very tart, add a small amount of agave. If you prefer a sharper drink, leave it out.

21+ only. Please enjoy responsibly and never drink and drive.
