Cocktail Recipe

How to Make a Classic Old Fashioned

Paola Market • Paola, Kansas

The Old Fashioned is one of the simplest whiskey cocktails, which means each ingredient matters. The goal is balance: spirit-forward, lightly sweet, aromatic, and cold.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz bourbon or rye whiskey
  • 2–3 dashes aromatic bitters
  • 1 tsp simple syrup, or to taste
  • Orange peel
  • Ice

Method

  1. Add whiskey, bitters, and simple syrup to a mixing glass with ice.
  2. Stir until chilled.
  3. Strain over fresh ice in a rocks glass.
  4. Express an orange peel over the drink and use it as garnish.

Make it fit your taste

Prefer it drier? Use less syrup. Want a brighter citrus aroma? Use a fresh orange peel. The classic structure is flexible enough to fine-tune without losing its identity.
