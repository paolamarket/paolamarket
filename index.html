Quick answers

Paola Market FAQ.

Everything you need before you head over.

Where is Paola Market located?+

We are at 304 Baptiste Dr, Paola, KS. Use the Google Maps section below for directions.

What are your store hours?+

Monday through Saturday we are open 10 AM–10 PM. Sunday we are open 12 PM–8 PM. Holiday hours may vary.

What do you carry?+

Paola Market offers beer, wine, spirits, tobacco, cigars, smoking accessories, and more for adult customers 21+.

Can I call to ask whether you have a specific item?+

Yes. Call us at (913) 283-4560 and ask about current availability before your visit.

Can Paola Market special-order a product for me?+

Yes. If the item is available through our distributors, we can try to special-order it for you. Call (913) 283-4560 or ask our team in store.

Does Paola Market have a drive-thru?+

Yes. We have a drive-thru option for convenient adult purchases. Visit us at 304 Baptiste Dr in Paola.

Can I place a larger order for a party or event?+

Yes. Call ahead and our team can help with larger beer, wine, spirits, mixer, and cigar needs for parties and events.

Do you host tastings?+

Yes, we plan to host select in-store tasting events. View our Tastings section for upcoming dates and details, or follow us on Facebook and Instagram for updates.

Do you have drink recipes and buying guides?+

Yes. Visit The Paola Pour for cocktail recipes, mixed-drink recipes, wine and spirit guides, pairings, and entertaining ideas.
